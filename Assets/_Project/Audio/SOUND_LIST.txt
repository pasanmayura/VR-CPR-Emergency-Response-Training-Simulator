CPR VR - SOUND FILES (world sounds only, no voice / instruction / phone sounds)
================================================================================
Put each file in its folder with EXACTLY this name (.wav, .ogg or .mp3 are all fine).
Then in Unity: Tools > Pavithra > Setup Spatial Audio  (it reports anything missing).
Prefer mono files: Unity places mono sounds in 3D best.

Ambience/   (seamless loops, 30 s or longer)
  ambience_room_hum          air conditioner / ventilation hum
  ambience_traffic_outside   distant city traffic (heard through the window)
  ambience_wall_clock        clock ticking
  ambience_light_buzz        fluorescent ceiling light buzz (very quiet)
  ambience_dog_barking       a dog barking a few times (2-5 s, NOT a loop: it is
                             repeated with random pauses, behind the room)
  ambience_wind_leaves       wind blowing through tree leaves (seamless loop)
  ambience_birds             birds chirping in a tree (seamless loop)

Vehicles/
  vehicle_three_wheeler_engine   three-wheeler / tuk-tuk / auto-rickshaw engine
                                 DRIVING at a steady speed (seamless loop, 5 s or more).
                                 Not a "pass-by" recording: Unity adds the Doppler
                                 effect itself while it drives along the street.

Footsteps/   (the player's own steps on the room's tiled floor, one step each, under 0.5 s)
  footstep_floor_01          shoe step on a hard tile / concrete floor
  footstep_floor_02          (four slightly different steps, so they never repeat)
  footstep_floor_03
  footstep_floor_04

Touch/   (short, under 1 s)
  touch_shoulder_tap         hand tapping a shoulder through clothing
  touch_skin                 soft hand-on-skin / cloth rustle

CPR/   (short, under 0.5 s, three slightly different takes)
  cpr_compression_01         dull chest press thud
  cpr_compression_02
  cpr_compression_03

AED/
  aed_pad_peel               sticker / adhesive peel
  aed_pad_place              pad pressed onto the chest (soft slap)
  aed_charge                 rising electronic charging whine (2-4 s)
  aed_shock                  defibrillator shock "thump" / electric zap

Ambulance/
  ambulance_siren_loop       siren, seamless loop
  ambulance_brake            vehicle braking / stopping
  ambulance_door             van door opening
  ambulance_footsteps        2-3 people walking in quickly
